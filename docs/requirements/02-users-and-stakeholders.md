# 02 · Users and Stakeholders

Status: `draft`

| ID | Stakeholder | Role | What they need from Padma |
|---|---|---|---|
| ST-01 | **Wearer** | Primary user; urban woman commuting, working or studying in a metro | Works without her doing anything; few false alarms; discreet; comfortable; easy to charge |
| ST-02 | **112 / ERSS operator** | Receives the alert and dispatches help | Clear signal that it's an emergency; accurate, updating location; a voice channel where possible; few false alerts |
| ST-03 | **Field responders** (police PCR, ambulance) | Reach the wearer | Location good enough to find a person (≤ ~10–20 m), updated while she moves |
| ST-04 | **Family / trusted contacts** | Secondary notification (deferred, see OQ-03) | Know that something happened and where |
| ST-05 | **Telecom operator** (Jio / Airtel / Vi) | Provides SIM, data, voice (VoLTE) and SMS | Certified module; SIM / eSIM provisioning |
| ST-06 | **Regulators** | DoT/TEC (MTCTE), WPC (radio), BIS, MeitY (DPDP Act) | Compliance before sale |
| ST-07 | **Builders / contributors** | Open-source community | Buildable, documented, hackable design |

## Primary persona (draft)

**Ananya, 24, Delhi NCR.** Commutes by metro, then shared auto or cab, sometimes late. Her phone is in her bag or hand. She wants something she can forget she's wearing, that won't embarrass her with false alarms, and that will act on its own if she can't.

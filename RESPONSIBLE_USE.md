# Responsible Use

Padma GPS exists to help people, especially women, **stay safe**. It is designed to be carried and controlled by the person it protects.

## Intended use

- A person carrying their own device and choosing who to share their location with.
- Triggering an SOS alert to trusted contacts in an emergency.
- Research, education, and building other personal safety products.

## Not acceptable

- Tracking any person **without their knowledge and consent**.
- Hiding the device on a person, in their bag, or in their vehicle.
- Any use for stalking, harassment, trafficking, or other crimes.

Covert tracking of a person is illegal in many countries. The Padma project does not support it in any form.

## Anti-stalking by design

The official Padma firmware is designed to make misuse harder:

- Location sharing is controlled by the person carrying the device.
- No hidden mode: the device gives a visible or physical signal when tracking or sharing.
- Planned support for detecting unwanted trackers on phones, using the industry *Detecting Unwanted Location Trackers* (DULT) standard.

Firmware modified to remove these protections is not Padma firmware.
